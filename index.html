History of Sarozai

Sarozai is a beautiful village with a historical background located in District Hangu, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. Sarozai is a land of green fields, beautiful rivers, and clear springs, along with high mountains. This region is rich in beautiful scenery, forests, springs, and mineral wealth.
