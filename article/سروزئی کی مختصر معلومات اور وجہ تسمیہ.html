Sarozai and Its Historical Background

Sarozai is a beautiful village with a historical background located in District Hangu, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

Sarozai is a land of green fields, beautiful rivers, and clear springs, along with high mountains. This region is rich in beautiful scenery, forests, springs, and mineral wealth.

It also has the distinction that, from the very beginning, this area has been connected to Hangu and other regions by road, whereas apart from this it is also cut off from the road at Karabad.

Sarozai, which is on the road at Karabad, connects directly by road to the Afghanistan border. From the beginning up until now, Pak-Afghan trade and travel occurs through this route.

The distance from Sarozai to Afghanistan is 48 miles.

Because Afghanistan is located on the border of Hindustan and Iran, the government which conquered this territory — whether the ruler of Hindustan or Iran, or even Alexander the Great from that point in history onward — if we look at this land through the lens of history, Hindustani, Iranian, Greek, Tatar, Seljuk, Mongol, and English rulers have all remained busy contesting this land through war.

Origin of the Name Sarozai

In earlier times, caravans used to travel back and forth for Pak-Afghan trade by means of camels.

These caravans used to camp here alongside the springs flowing from the foothills of the Sarozai mountains.

This place, because of its dense forests, was a shaded and cool area. For this reason, travelling caravans used to stop here. It was also essential for them to rest here.

At that time, there were plentiful trees and springs here. Those caravans used to make camp in the shade of these trees.

Hence, the people gave this place the name "سیروزائے" meaning "Shadow Place" — that is, "shady place."

From "سیروزائے," it later became "سَر وزئی".

Population and People of Sarozai

Sarozai has a population of 16,560 people.

This region's people possess personal strength, skill, courage, and are engaged in defense-field services outside the area as well, since the country benefits greatly from this exchange of skills.

Most of these people work abroad in foreign countries and Gulf states, providing services, since this is also a major exchange source of income for the country.

Boundaries of Sarozai

Sarozai's borders touch:

East: Kai and Muhammad Khwaja

North: Zayab

South: Boghz Sharif

According to records of 1875 AD, Sarozai's western boundary reaches the point of Nizha Cheena stream.

The Bangash People of Sarozai

Here Bangash tribe resides.

These people are civilized and cultured, and belong to one nation and lineage, and they all follow the same religion together, and they recognize each other well.

All the people of Sarozai live together like siblings.

No matter how much disagreement there may be over their family names' origin, there is no disagreement regarding them all being from one ancestor.

From the very beginning, these people have shown bravery, courage, dignity, struggle, and love of freedom with sincerity.

Community Life and Traditions

The people of Sarozai are, in each other's grief and joy, very brave and sympathetic toward one another.

Old, young, men and women — all take part in each other's grief and happiness.

They try to advance together with one another. Our traditions are very strong in these matters.

If any illness occurs in someone's home, or if someone passes away, then all the men of the village share in that grief equally, and they take part in whichever house the grief has occurred in.

They extend hospitality to guests — the people of the whole village together.

They also help with burial and shrouding.

In a grieving household, people do not experience any kind of hardship alone.

Because of people's human sympathy and good principles, our village is very famous for this, and this old custom has remained intact from ancient times to today.

Besides this, they also keep faith and religious commitment alongside outside help toward people.

These people do not forget favors done to them; it is necessary to gain their trust in order for them to be certain of your friendship.

War, grief, and joy — in every occasion — they help their friends or the rich and poor, without making any distinction.

When someone gains their trust, then this people's friendship becomes strong.

These people trust their friends with all sincerity.

These friends do not consider helping their friends a hardship, but rather they themselves take it upon themselves.

They share every secret with their friends.

When someone becomes friends with anyone, the whole family trusts them and maintains relations with them.
