Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026 • Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to Sarozai Village Platform (sarozaivillage.com). We respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding the personal information of our visitors. This Privacy Policy explains in clear, transparent terms how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you visit our website.

By accessing or using our website, you signify that you have read, understood, and agree to the terms described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please discontinue use of the website immediately.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in two primary ways: information you choose to provide directly to us, and information collected automatically through standard internet protocols and advertising technologies.

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Contact Form Submissions: When you communicate with us via our Contact Us page, we collect your name, email address, message subject, and message content to respond directly to your inquiry.
  • Comments & Community Feedback: Any voluntary suggestions, historical corrections, or photos submitted for archival consideration.

B. Automatically Collected Data & Server Log Files

Like virtually all standard web servers, our hosting system automatically records standard log files when you visit our site. These log files may include:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses (anonymized where required by law)
  • Browser type, version, and language preferences
  • Internet Service Provider (ISP)
  • Referring and exit pages, date/time stamps, and platform type
  • Aggregated click counts to analyze trends and optimize server response times

Log file information is never linked to personally identifiable information.

3. Cookies and Web Beacons

Sarozai Village uses cookies—small text files stored on your device—to enhance your browsing experience, store user preferences (such as light/dark mode selection), and analyze traffic patterns.

You have the option to accept or decline cookies through your individual browser settings. If you choose to disable cookies, some interactive features (such as theme persistence or interactive carousels) may experience minor visual limitations.

4. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Vendors

To support the ongoing hosting, maintenance, and historical research efforts of this free educational website, we partner with Google AdSense and authorized third-party ad networks to display advertisements.

Important Disclosures Regarding Google DoubleClick DART Cookies:

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this website.
  • Google's use of the DART cookie enables it to serve targeted, interest-based ads to users based on their visit to this site and other websites across the internet.
  • Users may opt out of the use of the DART cookie by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy.
  • You can customize or opt out of personalized Google advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons in their respective advertisements and links that appear on our website, which are sent directly to your browser. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. Sarozai Village has no access to or control over these cookies used by third-party advertisers.

5. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is strictly used to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the speed and visual design of the website
  • Respond promptly and courteously to user questions and feedback
  • Prevent fraudulent activity, spam, and cyber threats
  • Understand demographic reading trends to publish better historical and cultural articles

We do NOT sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal contact details to any third-party marketing brokers under any circumstances.

6. Third-Party Links & External References

Our articles and educational resources may contain outbound links to external websites (such as universities, government education boards, historical archives, or mapping services). Sarozai Village is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or accuracy of external websites. We strongly encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit.

7. GDPR Compliance (Rights for European Economic Area Users)

If you reside within the European Economic Area (EEA), you are entitled to specific data protection rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including:

  • The Right to Access: You may request copies of any personal communications we hold.
  • The Right to Rectification: You may request corrections to any inaccurate data.
  • The Right to Erasure: You may request that we delete your submitted contact inquiries from our database.
  • The Right to Restrict or Object to Processing: You may object to data processing activities under certain conditions.

8. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents have the right to know what personal data is collected, request its deletion, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. As stated, we do not sell your personal information.

9. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

Protecting the privacy of young children online is especially important to us. Sarozai Village is an educational and cultural platform that does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If a parent or guardian believes that a child under 13 has submitted contact details on our website, please notify us immediately, and we will promptly purge such records from our servers.

10. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in legal regulations, Google AdSense publisher policies, or new website features. The revised version will always be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date." We encourage visitors to check this page periodically.
